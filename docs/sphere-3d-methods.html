Drawing a Planet as a Sphere

Every method rendered on four planets at real card radii (r 24 / 16 / 10 / 6) over the card's dark ground. The constraint: it must read as round, differ from a flat disc, and not look aggressively lit from one side. Scroll and pick.

The unavoidable trade

A circle only reads as a sphere because of a light→dark gradient across it. There is no method that adds volume without implying a light. Everything below is a point on that spectrum — from "barely there" to "obviously lit".

For this card: methods 1–5 are one shared or per-colour gradient, free to draw. Method 6 (SVG filter) allocates a filter region per body every frame — measurable on HA tablets. Method 8 is a bitmap in a vector card — blurs on zoom.
